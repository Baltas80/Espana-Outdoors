# España Outdoor — Product Principles

## Purpose
España Outdoor is an outdoor platform for Spain combining maps, route planning, live context, safety, pets, wildlife, conservation and emergency support.

## Differentiation
The product must not become a simple route catalogue. Every major experience should add context: current conditions, hazards, suitability, offline readiness, environmental sensitivity and actionable safety information.

## Trust hierarchy
1. Official source
2. Verified source
3. Community report
4. Estimate/AI assistance

Never present an estimate or AI inference as an official fact.

## Freshness
Dynamic information must expose source and update time. Stale data must be visibly marked and must not silently appear current.

## Offline-first
Critical route and safety information should remain useful without connectivity. Sync must be resumable and failure tolerant.

## Emergency principle
Official emergency services take precedence. Community assistance is supplementary and must never encourage unsafe intervention.

## Privacy
Collect the minimum data necessary. Exact location is especially sensitive. Sharing must be explicit, temporary, revocable and purpose-limited.

## Wildlife
Do not expose sensitive locations that could facilitate disturbance or exploitation of vulnerable species.

## Design
Outdoor readability, one-handed use, accessibility, dark mode and degraded/offline states are first-class requirements.
