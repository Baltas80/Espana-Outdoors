# España Outdoor — Brand Direction v0.1

## Chosen creative territory
The visual identity should combine Spanish identity, outdoor exploration and technology without becoming a tourism logo or a generic hiking app.

Two visual territories are being explored:

1. **Summit Mark** — geometric natural landscape symbol with subtle Spanish red/gold accents.
2. **Ruta España** — a continuous trail/path forming the core symbol, with red and gold integrated into the route.

The second territory is intentionally different and should be evaluated as a serious alternative rather than a variation of the first.

## Brand attributes
- Premium
- Contemporary
- Outdoor
- Trustworthy
- Technological
- Human
- Safe
- Conservation-minded
- Spanish, but internationally legible

## Spanish identity
Spanish red and gold are accents, not a literal flag treatment. Avoid making the flag the dominant graphic device. The identity should remain recognisable in monochrome.

## Principal visual direction
- Forest/deep green foundation
- Warm neutral surfaces
- Spanish red and gold accents
- High-contrast typography
- Clean geometric iconography
- Photography based on real Spanish landscapes and wildlife

## UI implications
The brand system must work in light and dark modes and under difficult outdoor viewing conditions. Safety states must be immediately distinguishable from ordinary content.

## Do not imitate
The visual system must not resemble Wikiloc, Komoot, AllTrails or another established outdoor navigation brand. Do not reuse their distinctive visual language, layouts or iconography.

## Required brand deliverables
- Symbol
- Wordmark
- Horizontal and vertical lockups
- App icon
- Monochrome versions
- Dark/light variants
- Colour tokens
- Typography tokens
- Icon system
- Photography direction
- UI component examples
- SOS/Rescue Link visual language

## Tagline candidate
**Explora España. Hazlo preparado.**

This is a working direction, not a final locked logo. Final selection should be made after comparing multiple coherent concepts at app-icon size, UI size and full brand scale.
